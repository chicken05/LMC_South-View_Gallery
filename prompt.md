Create a visually immersive, scroll-driven interactive website for **South-View Cemetery's African American history**, inspired by [SBS's The Boat](https://www.sbs.com.au/theboat/).

### Core Concept
The website should feel like an illustrated historical journey through Atlanta, beginning with South-View Cemetery's founding in 1886 and progressing through the Civil Rights Movement into the present.

### Design & Animations
- **Dynamic background:** An illustrated Atlanta skyline that evolves historically as users scroll. Begin with a monochrome pencil/stencil sketch of 1880s Atlanta, gradually introducing color, buildings, and detail as time progresses into modern Atlanta.
- Use layered parallax effects, subtle animations, and smooth visual transitions to create an immersive experience similar to *The Boat*.
- The skyline should genuinely reflect Atlanta's architectural changes, not simply recolor the same buildings.
- Use an artistic, hand-drawn aesthetic that gradually becomes more vibrant, while maintaining visual consistency.
- The background should transition continuously rather than abruptly between scenes.

### Three Main Sections
1. **Founding & Early History (founding–1940s):** South-View's establishment, segregation, and early African American history. Mostly pencil sketches and muted colors.
2. **Civil Rights Era (1950s–1960s):** Civil Rights leaders, movements, and events connected to South-View. Gradually introduce more color and visual complexity.
3. **Post-Civil Rights (1970s–Present):** Legacy, community impact, and modern Atlanta. Transition to a fully colored, vibrant skyline.

### Foreground Timeline
- Display historical entries as users scroll, using elegant editorial layouts with dates, titles, short descriptions, and archival photographs/documents.
- Alternate image and text positioning to keep the experience visually interesting.
- Use subtle fade, slide, and parallax animations for entries.
- Include a persistent, unobtrusive timeline indicator showing the current year and historical period.
- Make entries easy to add, remove, or edit using a structured data file.
- Focus on historically verified people and events connected to South-View Cemetery. Use clearly labeled placeholders where researched content or archival images are not yet available.

### Technical Requirements
- **Mobile-first and fully responsive**, including touch scrolling.
- Smooth, performant scroll animations without excessive loading or jank.
- Support reduced-motion accessibility preferences.
- Design it as a standalone website that can be hosted externally and embedded into **Wix using an iframe**.
- Keep content separate from animation/design code so our team can easily expand the timeline.

### Overall Direction
Prioritize beautiful visual storytelling over a conventional timeline website. The experience should feel like moving through a living, illustrated history of Atlanta, with South-View Cemetery and its historical figures at the center.

Build a working prototype with the three historical sections, representative timeline-entry placeholders, skyline transitions, and functional responsive animations.
